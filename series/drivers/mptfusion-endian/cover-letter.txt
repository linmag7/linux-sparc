Decode little-endian cached firmware fields before host calculations and
PIO reload. This generic driver correction applies independently to
v7.3-rc1; PRIMEPOWER reset/reload tests motivated it.

The exact cleaned integration booted PRIMEPOWER UP/SMP from disk to
Gentoo with an ext4 root. Reset and firmware-reload evidence remains
from earlier images; those operations were not repeated in this final
qualification. Tests used the integrated stack, not this patch alone.

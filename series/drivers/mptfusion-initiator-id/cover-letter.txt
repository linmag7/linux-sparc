Restore the firmware-provided PRIMEPOWER SPI initiator identity in the
shared initialization path before PortEnable, including reset and resume.
This driver patch applies independently to v7.3-rc1; it is separate from
the generic firmware-endian correction and platform CPU/IRQ support.

The exact cleaned integration booted PRIMEPOWER UP/SMP from disk to
Gentoo with an ext4 root. Reset/resume and cold-boot identity evidence
remain from earlier images; those cases were not repeated in this final
qualification. Tests used the integrated stack, not this patch alone.

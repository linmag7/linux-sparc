Subject: [PATCH 0/1] sparc32: serialize SuperSPARC demap operations

I am posting this as a separate companion to the generic SPARC32 cache
and TLB fixes. It serializes complete sun4m Viking shootdowns at their
initiator, using synchronous singleton cross-calls and lock-free remote
callbacks. It retains the accumulated mm CPU mask.

The patch applies before or after the generic series, producing the
same source tree. SMP/UP builds and QEMU SS-20 fork/COW smoke tests
passed with the separate PIL/IPI prerequisites. Physical SuperSPARC
validation remains pending. The Viking instruction-cache change is
outside this patch.

Codex assisted with rebase, review, validation and submission preparation.
